what I do, in plain words

Network & system administration
Building and running the networks and servers an organization depends on: connecting the machines, managing accounts and email, keeping everything working. This is what I was trained for.
Penetration testing
Testing the security of a system with its owner's permission, the way a locksmith checks whether your locks can be picked. The goal is to find the weak points before a real attacker does.
Reverse engineering
Taking a program apart to understand how it works when nobody gives you the plans. It is how you find out what unknown or suspicious software really does.
Binary exploitation
Finding programming mistakes inside software and showing how they could be abused, so they can be corrected.
